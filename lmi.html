Workforce Need & Demand

Labor Market Data

The Orange County Center of Excellence completed a May 2026 labor market assessment for the proposed degree and issued a positive labor market endorsement for the program concept.

Key Findings

  • Regional labor market data indicate a supply-demand gap for workers with a bachelor’s degree aligned to Applied Human-AI Collaboration and Leadership.
  • BLS and job posting data show the target occupations typically require or prefer a bachelor’s degree.
  • Of 3,132 aligned postings that list education, 2,766 requested a bachelor’s degree.
  • Entry-level wages for the target occupations exceed the Orange County and Los Angeles single-adult living wage benchmarks.
  • Available posting data show bachelor’s wage premiums for Project Management Specialists and Management Analysts.

Demand, Supply, and Wages

Core evidence from the OC COE report.

California Outlook

The three target occupations are projected to grow 6% statewide from 2024 to 2034, with 50,414 annual openings.

Regional Outlook

Los Angeles and Orange counties are projected to grow 3% from 2024 to 2034, with 15,835 annual openings.

Regional Supply

Related non-community college baccalaureate awards averaged 11,479 annually across LA/OC from 2021 to 2023.

Employer Education Signal

Among aligned postings with education data, 88% requested a bachelor’s degree, compared with 3% requesting an associate degree.

Orange County Wages

Entry-level hourly wages range from $34.98 to $40.33, above the $27.13 single-adult living wage benchmark.

Los Angeles Wages

Entry-level hourly wages range from $36.33 to $40.49, above the $24.03 single-adult living wage benchmark.

Conclusion
