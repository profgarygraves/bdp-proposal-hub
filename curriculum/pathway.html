Map Notes

  • Restricted electives: complete all 18 units, choosing a different course each time from MKT 256 F, CIS 210 F, CIS 235 F, CYBR 256 F, BUS 180 F, and CISG 105 F.
  • Cal-GETC: students complete the Cal-GETC pattern in Years 1–2. STAT C1000 meets both a major requirement and Cal-GETC Area 2.
  • Upper-division sequence: take HAI 300 in the first upper-division term. HAI 350 and HAI 370 must be completed with a grade of C or better before HAI 495A, and HAI 495A before HAI 495B.
  • Upper-division general education: ENGL 301 F and PHIL 361 F.
  • Units: Cal-GETC areas are shown at 3 units. Some approved courses carry 4 units (for example, many English composition and science courses); students who choose them need fewer elective units to reach 120.
