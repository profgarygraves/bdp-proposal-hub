How the bachelor's fee works

  • Lower-division courses (the AS and the restricted electives) cost the standard $46 per unit.
  • Upper-division courses (HAI 300-495B, BUS 371, ENGL 301 F, PHIL 361 F) cost $130 per unit: the $46 enrollment fee plus an $84 baccalaureate fee required by California Education Code § 78042(g)(5).
  • The 40 upper-division units total $5,200 in enrollment fees.

Financial aid

  • California College Promise Grant (CCPG): waives the $46 per unit enrollment fee for eligible students, including upper-division units. It does not cover the $84 baccalaureate fee, so an eligible student would pay about $3,360 in fees for the 40 upper-division units.
  • Federal Pell Grant, Cal Grant, and Middle Class Scholarship: baccalaureate students may qualify. Complete the FAFSA or California Dream Act Application each year and meet the March 2 Cal Grant deadline.
  • Payment plans are available for fees not covered by financial aid.
  • Meet with the Fullerton College Financial Aid Office to confirm eligibility.

Comparison

For the junior and senior years, CSU systemwide tuition for 2026-2027 is $6,838 per year for full-time undergraduates, or about $13,676 for two years, before campus fees. The same two years in this program cost an estimated $6,028 in enrollment fees.
