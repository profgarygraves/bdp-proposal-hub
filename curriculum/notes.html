Ideas and Open Questions Cross-discipline partnerships for the HAI courses

Draft thinking, not a proposal. This page is not in the site menu and asks search engines not to index it, but the repository is public, so anyone with the link can read it. Last updated 2026-10-09.

The idea

Several upper-division courses could be housed in, cross-listed with, or co-taught by disciplines outside Business. Each partner discipline then has a stake in the degree, and the program needs only one faculty champion per division. ENGL 301 F (Humanities) and PHIL 361 F (Humanities) already work this way.

Second benefit: Title 5 requires at least 9 units of upper-division general education from at least two disciplines outside the major, one emphasizing written communication, oral communication, or computation. ENGL 301 F and PHIL 361 F supply 6. A third course housed in a non-Business discipline (for example, Statistics for computation, or Psychology) could close the 3-unit gap that BUS 371 may not cover. Confirm with the Articulation Officer.

Three ways to bring a discipline in

  1. Add disciplines (lightest touch). Keep the HAI prefix but list additional disciplines in the course's State Discipline (FSA) field so qualified faculty from that discipline can teach it. No new course needed.
  2. Cross-list. Two identical course outlines with different prefixes (for example, HAI 320 F and STAT 320 F). Per the PCAH, every element except subject code and number must match, including the SAM code. Students take one or the other.
  3. Re-house. The partner department owns the course outright (for example, STAT 3xx F). Strongest buy-in, but the program depends on that department scheduling it.

Things to check before proposing

  • Faculty qualifications: upper-division courses need a master's degree in the discipline (Title 5 § 53410). Confirm each partner department has qualified faculty interested in teaching.
  • Course numbers in the state application: the CCCCO application lists HAI course numbers. Ask the Chancellor's Office whether prefix changes need notification during intersegmental review.
  • Academic Senate purview: discipline assignment is a 10+1 matter. Bring the idea to the department chairs and the Curriculum Committee early.
  • Load and FTES: partner departments earn the FTES for courses they own, which is an incentive worth stating.
  • Cypress College: NOCCCD encourages alignment between colleges; check for related Cypress courses.
  • Upper-division GE: confirm with Articulation which re-housed courses could count toward the 9 units.

Possible champions by division

  • Humanities: ENGL 301 F and PHIL 361 F are already in the program.
  • Math & Computer Science: HAI 320 (Statistics).
  • Social Sciences: HAI 360 (Law, Public Policy, and Society or Political Science), BUS 371 (Psychology), HAI 310 (Economics).
  • Fine Arts: HAI 380 (Graphic Design or Digital Arts).
  • Business/CIS: program home; HAI 370 (CIS).
