2Program Description

The Bachelor of Science in Applied Human-AI Collaboration and Leadership prepares students to lead, implement, and govern the integration of artificial intelligence within organizations. Unlike traditional artificial intelligence programs focused primarily on software development, machine learning, or technical system design, this program emphasizes the human, organizational, and strategic dimensions of AI adoption and implementation.

Students learn how to collaborate effectively with AI systems to enhance decision-making, improve productivity, redesign workflows, and drive innovation across industries. The program integrates applied AI literacy, organizational leadership, data-informed decision-making, responsible AI governance, and digital transformation strategies, preparing graduates to bridge technical teams, business stakeholders, and organizational leaders.

Graduates will be equipped to guide AI-enabled change, manage human-AI collaboration, evaluate ethical and organizational implications of AI technologies, and develop strategies that align artificial intelligence with business objectives, workforce needs, and long-term organizational success. This degree requires 86 units, in addition to other graduation requirements.

3Program Justification

The Bachelor of Science in Applied Human-AI Collaboration and Leadership addresses a rapidly growing workforce need for professionals who can lead the adoption, implementation, and governance of artificial intelligence within organizations. As AI becomes integrated across business functions, employers increasingly seek individuals who can bridge technical teams and business stakeholders, manage organizational change, support data-informed decision-making, and ensure the responsible use of AI technologies. The Orange County Center of Excellence labor market assessment identified strong demand for occupations aligned with the proposed degree, including General and Operations Managers, Project Management Specialists, and Management Analysts, and concluded that employers overwhelmingly prefer candidates with bachelor's degrees for these AI-enabled business and leadership roles. By combining organizational leadership, AI implementation, responsible AI governance, and digital transformation strategies, the program prepares graduates to meet a critical and emerging workforce need not adequately addressed by traditional business or technical AI degree pathways.

4Program Level Student Learning Outcomes and Assessments

  1. Evaluate, implement, and manage AI-enabled solutions that improve organizational effectiveness, decision-making, innovation, and operational performance while integrating the needs of people, processes, and technology.Assessment: Capstone Project
  2. Lead organizational change and digital transformation initiatives by facilitating collaboration among technical teams, business stakeholders, employees, and organizational leaders during AI adoption and integration efforts.Assessment: Project
  3. Apply ethical, legal, regulatory, and societal considerations to develop, evaluate, and govern responsible AI practices that promote transparency, accountability, equity, privacy, security, and organizational trust.Assessment: Case Analysis / Exam
  4. Develop and communicate AI strategies that align organizational goals, workforce needs, data-informed decision-making, and emerging technologies to create sustainable competitive advantage and business innovation.Assessment: Oral Presentation

Assessment methods are proposed drafts for review.
