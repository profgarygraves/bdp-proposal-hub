What We Are Proposing

A practical bachelor’s pathway for the AI-enabled workplace.

The proposed Applied Human-AI Collaboration & Leadership BS would prepare students to work across industries where AI is becoming part of everyday planning, analysis, communication, operations, management, and strategy. The focus is applying AI in real organizations: helping students understand what AI can do, evaluate its outputs, redesign human workflows around it, and lead teams through responsible adoption.

01

The Degree

A Bachelor of Science built around applied AI for business leadership, business data, human-AI collaboration, ethics, organizational innovation, and leadership.

02

The Student

Students who may not want to become software engineers, but do want to lead, analyze, manage, communicate, and solve problems in AI-enabled environments.

03

The Skills

Students would learn to apply AI tools, interpret data, design better workflows, evaluate risk and bias, communicate insights, and guide implementation.

04

The Need

Employers increasingly need workers who can bridge technology and human judgment: people who understand AI well enough to use it responsibly and lead others through change.
