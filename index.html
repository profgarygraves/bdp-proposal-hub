What We Are Proposing

A practical bachelor’s pathway for the AI-enabled workplace.

The proposed Applied Human-AI Collaboration & Leadership BS would prepare students to work across industries where AI is becoming part of everyday planning, analysis, communication, operations, management, and strategy. The focus is applying AI in real organizations: helping students understand what AI can do, evaluate its outputs, redesign human workflows around it, and lead teams through responsible adoption.

01

The Degree

A Bachelor of Science built around applied AI for business leadership, business data, human-AI collaboration, ethics, organizational innovation, and leadership.

02

The Student

Students who may not want to become software engineers, but do want to lead, analyze, manage, communicate, and solve problems in AI-enabled environments.

03

The Skills

Students would learn to apply AI tools, interpret data, design better workflows, evaluate risk and bias, communicate insights, and guide implementation.

04

The Need

Employers increasingly need workers who can bridge technology and human judgment: people who understand AI well enough to use it responsibly and lead others through change.

Current Status

Provisional approval granted; intersegmental review is underway.

On October 2, 2026, the Chancellor's Office announced that the proposed degree passed the program compliance and quality review and was granted provisional approval. The proposal now moves to intersegmental consultation, where CSU, UC, and AICCU review it for duplication.

Quality Review Passed

The application passed policy and compliance review and scored 136.5 of 150 possible points in the Cycle 9 BDP quality review.

Provisional Approval

The Chancellor's Office granted provisional approval on October 2, 2026.

Intersegmental Review

CSU, UC, and AICCU review runs October 2 – November 13, with any campus-to-campus consultations November 13–30.

Remaining Approvals

Full approval also requires ACCJC substantive change approval, now in progress, and California Community Colleges Board of Governors approval.

Status last updated October 2026.

CCCCO Vision 2030

Advancing access, success, support, and economic mobility.

The proposed degree connects all three Vision 2030 goals with all three strategic directions. It expands an affordable community college baccalaureate pathway, responds to regional workforce demand, and prepares students to use and lead with AI responsibly.

Equitable Baccalaureate Attainment

A structured 2+2 pathway from associate preparation to a community college bachelor's degree supports Vision 2030's completion and baccalaureate attainment outcomes.

Workforce & Economic Development

Regional labor market evidence connects degree completion to high-demand occupations, bachelor-level hiring expectations, and wages above regional living-wage benchmarks.

Generative AI & the Future of Learning

The curriculum builds AI literacy, human oversight, ethical judgment, governance, and the ability to apply AI responsibly in real organizations.
