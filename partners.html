Employer Outreach Letter

Message being shared with employers and advisory partners.

Hello,

Fullerton College’s Business Management Department is currently exploring the development of a new Bachelor of Science degree in Applied Human-AI Collaboration and Leadership.

California Community Colleges are now authorized under recent revisions to California Title 5 regulations to offer select applied and career-focused bachelor’s degrees that address regional workforce needs and workforce shortages. These programs are intended to expand affordable access to high-quality undergraduate education in specialized workforce and Career Technical Education fields.

The proposed Applied Human-AI Collaboration and Leadership BS degree is designed to prepare graduates to effectively implement, manage, lead, and collaborate with artificial intelligence technologies across business operations, organizational innovation, workflow optimization, project management, and AI-enabled decision-making environments.

This is not a computer science or software engineering degree. The focus is applied business leadership, organizational effectiveness, AI-assisted workflows, and helping organizations adapt to rapidly evolving AI technologies.

As part of the California Community Colleges Chancellor’s Office bachelor’s degree approval process, we are conducting a regional employer workforce survey to better understand current and future AI-related workforce needs, desired workforce skills and competencies, hiring trends and workforce gaps, and employer perspectives on bachelor-level preparation in this emerging field.

Recent labor market research conducted by the Orange County Center of Excellence identified strong projected workforce growth in related occupations, significant employer preference for bachelor-level preparation, increasing demand for professionals who can bridge business leadership and applied AI implementation, and expanding need for human-AI collaboration skills across industries.

The survey takes approximately 5 to 8 minutes to complete, and your feedback would be greatly appreciated.
