Advisory Board

The advisory board should include industry professionals, AI practitioners, business leaders, operations managers, analytics leaders, and workforce development partners.

  • Validate workforce need and job role alignment
  • Review proposed learning outcomes and course sequence
  • Advise on applied tools, ethics, and workplace expectations
  • Support internships, projects, guest lectures, and employer letters

Workforce Alignment

The program concept should be reviewed with employers, workforce boards, and regional economic development partners to demonstrate relevance and non-duplication.

  • Employers using AI-enabled workflows
  • Regional workforce development boards
  • Orange County and Southern California economic partners
  • Regional consortium representatives
