Now · Parallel Approvals

State review and local approvals move forward at the same time.

CCCCO Quality Review and Provisional Approval

The application passed policy and compliance review, scored 136.5 of 150 points, and received provisional approval on October 2, 2026.

Intersegmental Duplication Review

CSU, UC, and AICCU review runs October 2 – November 13; written objections, if any, are due November 13, with campus-to-campus consultations November 13–30. Document all objections, program changes, and unresolved concerns.

FC Curriculum Committee Approval

Submit the program and all 11 new upper-division course outlines (HAI 300–380, BUS 371, HAI 495A/B), each with a Distance Education Addendum, to the Fullerton College Curriculum Committee. Review runs alongside intersegmental review rather than after it; any changes that come out of CSU/UC consultation are handled as normal course revisions. Local approval is needed for chaptering, the catalog, and the ACCJC application.

FC Faculty Senate Approval

Following Curriculum Committee action, the program goes to the Fullerton College Faculty Senate. Use this step to confirm discipline assignments for partner departments (10+1 purview) and to resolve the upper-division general education question with Articulation.

NOCCCD Board of Trustees Approval

With college-level approvals in place, the program and courses go to the North Orange County Community College District Board of Trustees. Schedule the vote after intersegmental consultation closes (November 30) or make it contingent on final state approval; confirm the district's preferred approach with the Dean and Vice President of Instruction.

ACCJC Substantive Change

The substantive change process was initiated in August 2026. Provide local approval dates as they occur. Once ACCJC approval is granted, submit the approval letter to BDP@cccco.edu. ACCJC approval is required for full program approval and implementation.

Next · Full State Approval

Final action by the Board of Governors.

Board of Governors Approval

After quality review, duplication review, and ACCJC approval are complete, the program goes to the Board President and the California Community Colleges Board of Governors for full approval.

Then · Student Access and Launch

Steps that must be finished before the first cohort can enroll and receive aid.

Federal Financial Aid (Title IV) Eligibility

A bachelor's degree is a new credential level for Fullerton College, so the U.S. Department of Education must approve the program before Pell Grants or federal loans can be disbursed to its students. After ACCJC and state approval, Financial Aid files the Electronic Application (E-App) to add the program to the college's Eligibility and Certification Approval Report. Also set up Cal Grant eligibility for baccalaureate students, upper-division loan limits and packaging for third- and fourth-year students, and satisfactory academic progress rules. ED review time varies, so start as soon as approvals allow. Owner: Financial Aid.

Chaptering, Catalog, and Scheduling

After local and state approval, submit the program and courses to the Chancellor's Office Curriculum Inventory for chaptering; complete catalog production, class scheduling, faculty assignments, and articulation for the first cohort.
