Program Design

Program Overview

The proposed 120-unit Bachelor of Science follows a 2+2 pathway from associate-level AI in Business preparation into upper-division study in applied AI for business leadership, organizational strategy, ethics, and human-AI collaboration.

Draft Program Description

Business leadership for applying AI in real organizations.

The Bachelor of Science in Applied Human-AI Collaboration and Leadership prepares students to lead, design, and manage the integration of artificial intelligence within organizations. Unlike traditional AI programs focused primarily on programming and technical development, this program emphasizes the human, organizational, and strategic dimensions of AI adoption.

Students learn how to work alongside AI systems to enhance decision-making, improve productivity, and drive innovation across industries. The program integrates applied AI literacy, data-informed decision-making, organizational leadership, and ethical governance, equipping graduates to bridge business and technology.

Learning Outcomes

Students will be able to:

Apply AI Concepts and Tools

Analyze and apply artificial intelligence concepts and tools to improve organizational decision-making, operational efficiency, and innovation.

Interpret AI-Generated Insights

Use data analytics and AI-generated insights to support strategic and operational decisions.

Lead AI-Enabled Change

Lead cross-functional teams and manage organizational change associated with AI adoption and integration.

Govern Responsible AI

Evaluate ethical, legal, and societal implications and implement responsible, equitable, and compliant AI practices.

Develop AI Strategy

Create AI adoption strategies aligned with business objectives, workforce needs, and industry trends.

Communicate Across Audiences

Communicate complex AI concepts and solutions effectively to technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Design Applied Solutions

Design, develop, and present a real-world AI-enabled solution that addresses a defined organizational challenge.

Bridge People and Technology

Connect technical teams, business stakeholders, users, and organizational leaders around practical AI implementation.

Lower Division

AI in Business Foundation

The lower-division component includes Cal-GETC and major preparation through the Artificial Intelligence in Business AS or equivalent preparation.

  • BUS 108 F - Living in an Online World
  • BUS 256 F - Artificial Intelligence and Prompt Engineering for Business
  • BUS 257 F - AI Applications for Business
  • CIS 201 F - Introduction to Python Programming
  • CIS 258 F - Applied AI: Machine Learning, Deep Learning and NLP

Upper Division

Human-AI Leadership Core

The upper-division component advances students into applied AI leadership, strategy, governance, project management, and human-centered interaction design.

  • HAI 300 - Foundations of Human-AI Collaboration
  • HAI 310 - Applied AI for Organizational Decision-Making
  • HAI 320 - Data Analytics and Visualization for Leaders
  • HAI 340 - Organizational Leadership in AI-Enabled Environments
  • HAI 350 - AI Strategy and Implementation
  • HAI 360 - Ethics, Governance, and Responsible AI
  • HAI 370 - Managing AI Projects and Cross-Functional Teams
  • HAI 380 - Human-AI Interaction and Experience Design
