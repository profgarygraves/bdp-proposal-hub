Student Demand Evidence

Student Interest

CCCCO application guidance asks for substantial student interest, clear enrollment and completer projections, and the basis for those projections. Student surveys are preferred.

Survey Evidence Needed

The workforce need template asks for the number of students surveyed, the response rate, and levels of student interest by percentage.

  • How interested are you in careers that use AI tools in business, analytics, operations, or leadership?
  • How likely would you be to enroll in an Applied Human-AI Collaboration & Leadership BS at Fullerton College?
  • Which career goals are most relevant to you?
  • What AI tools or workplace applications are you already using or interested in learning?
  • Would you like to receive updates about the proposed program?
  • What is your current program or major, and when do you expect to complete your associate degree or transfer preparation?

Evidence Goal

Use responses to summarize substantial student demand, likely enrollment, projected completers, career interests, and the basis for enrollment projections in the Question 8 workforce need narrative.
