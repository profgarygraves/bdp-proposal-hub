Student Demand Evidence

Student Interest

Student survey results and enrollment projections were included in the August 2026 CCCCO application as evidence of student demand.

Submitted Student Survey

The survey ran from May 12 through July 4, 2026 and received responses from 25 current students and alumni. Key findings included:

  • 76% were very or somewhat interested in the proposed program.
  • 56% said they would definitely or likely enroll.
  • 68% planned to pursue a bachelor's degree.
  • 80% rated AI skills as important or extremely important to their careers.
  • 40% said the program would influence their decision to remain at Fullerton College.

The embedded survey remains available for ongoing interest and stakeholder feedback. New responses were not part of the submitted Cycle 9 results.

Enrollment Outlook

The application projects an initial cohort of 25-35 students, annual program enrollment of 75-100 students within five years, and 20-30 annual completers at full implementation.
