Cycle 9 Application Path

Status & Timeline

The proposal began in November 2025, completed regional and evidence review during 2026, was submitted to the CCCCO in August 2026, and received provisional approval on October 2, 2026. It now moves through intersegmental duplication review, ACCJC approval, local approval by the FC Curriculum Committee, Faculty Senate, and NOCCCD Board of Trustees, and Board of Governors approval.

Nov. 2025 Planning process started
May 2026 COE LMI received
Aug. 2026 Cycle 9 application submitted
Oct. 2026 CCCCO provisional approval
Done

Labor Market Assessment

Planning began in November 2025. The Center of Excellence BDP labor market assessment was requested in February 2026 and has been received, including the labor market endorsement and required workforce data.

Done

Program and Curriculum Development

The final submission defines the 120-unit pathway, upper-division curriculum, four program learning outcomes, catalog description, Cal-GETC pathway, faculty qualifications, and two-course capstone.

Done

Regional Consultation

Employer and education partner evidence was collected, three workforce boards provided joint support, and the Orange County Regional Consortium recommended the proposal on June 16, 2026.

Done

Student Demand and Non-Duplication

Student survey results, enrollment and completer projections, and the statewide CSU/UC non-duplication analysis were completed and included in the application.

Done

Administrative and Funding Readiness

The administrative and funding plan, student support model, resource analysis, district reserve policy, and institutional certifications were included in the final package.

Done

CCCCO Cycle 9 Submission

The complete application and supporting attachments were submitted to the Chancellor's Office in August 2026, ahead of the Cycle 9 deadline.

Done

CCCCO Quality Review & Provisional Approval

On October 2, 2026, the Chancellor's Office announced that the application passed the program compliance and quality review, scoring 136.5 of 150 points, and granted the degree provisional approval.

Now

Intersegmental Duplication Review

The Chancellor's Office sent the application to CSU, UC, and AICCU for a 30-day review (October 2 – November 13). Any written objections are due November 13, followed by campus-to-campus consultations November 13–30. Fullerton College will document any concerns raised, changes made, and resolutions reached.

Now

ACCJC Substantive Change

Fullerton College initiated the ACCJC substantive change process in August 2026. Once ACCJC approval is granted, the approval letter is submitted to the Chancellor's Office. ACCJC approval is required for full program approval and implementation.

Next

FC Curriculum Committee Approval

The Fullerton College Curriculum Committee reviews and approves the program and its courses as part of the local curriculum approval process.

Next

Faculty Senate Approval

Following Curriculum Committee action, the program goes to the Fullerton College Faculty Senate for review and approval.

Next

NOCCCD Board of Trustees Approval

With college-level approvals in place, the program is brought to the North Orange County Community College District Board of Trustees for approval.

Next

Board of Governors Approval

Once the program clears quality review, duplication review, and ACCJC approval, it goes to the Board President and is brought forward to the California Community Colleges Board of Governors as part of the regular public reporting process.

Then

Implementation and Launch

After full approval, finalize local curriculum and catalog processes, confirm staffing and student supports, and carry out the launch and recruitment plan.

What needs attention during intersegmental review.

Consultation Record

Document every objection raised, any changes made to the program, and any unresolved duplication concerns, as the Chancellor's Office requires.

Accreditation

Complete the ACCJC substantive change review and forward the approval letter to the Chancellor's Office.

Launch Readiness

Refine curriculum, staffing, advising, student support, communications, and enrollment plans ahead of full approval.

Regulatory Design Checks

Title 5 requirements to keep visible through review and implementation.

120 Units

Baccalaureate degrees require completion of at least 120 semester units.

40 Upper-Division Units

The degree must include at least 40 semester units of total upper-division coursework.

9 Upper-Division GE Units

Upper-division general education must include at least nine semester units across at least two disciplines outside the major.

Program Admission

Enrollment in upper-division courses is limited to students admitted to a California community college baccalaureate program.

Source: CCCCO BDP application materials, workforce need overview, and March 2025 final revisions to Title 5 baccalaureate degree regulations.
