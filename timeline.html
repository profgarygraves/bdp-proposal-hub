Regulatory Design Checks

Title 5 requirements to keep visible through review and implementation.

120 Units

Baccalaureate degrees require completion of at least 120 semester units.

40 Upper-Division Units

The degree must include at least 40 semester units of total upper-division coursework.

9 Upper-Division GE Units

Upper-division general education must include at least nine semester units across at least two disciplines outside the major.

Program Admission

Enrollment in upper-division courses is limited to students admitted to a California community college baccalaureate program.

Source: CCCCO BDP application materials, workforce need overview, and March 2025 final revisions to Title 5 baccalaureate degree regulations.
